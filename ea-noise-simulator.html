Model notes
The spectrum shows jammer power at the amplifier output and each radar as received at the jammer antenna. J/S uses free-space spreading with fixed antenna gains and treats noise as white inside the radar band; the atmosphere, multipath, horizon, RCS fluctuation and coherent (DRFM) jamming are not modelled. Saturated summing uses a memoryless envelope limiter on constant-envelope (FM noise) channels, tabulated by Monte-Carlo up to 16 channels; products above 5th order are counted in the total but not drawn. With more than 6 summed channels only 3rd-order products are drawn. Sum, Mux and Sum+Mux are the simulator's names for simultaneous, time-multiplexed and grouped power sharing. Educational model, not a measured system.
